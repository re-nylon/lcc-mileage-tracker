    Send trips to Oracle

    Copy your trips here, then let the bookmark fill in the Oracle form one trip at a time. You check and submit each one.

    Log trips on your phone as you go. The Oracle bookmark works on your work computer, so do this part there. Move your trips across with Download backup and Restore backup in Settings.

    How it works

    1. Once only: drag this button onto your browser's bookmarks bar.
      Fill Oracle Mileage
      Can't drag it? Right-click it and choose Bookmark link or Add to favourites.
    2. Click Copy trips waiting to go into Oracle.
    3. In Oracle, open a new Mileage Personal Contribution.
    4. Click the Fill Oracle Mileage bookmark. The first time, it loads the trips you copied.
    5. Check the fields, then click Continue and Submit in Oracle. The bookmark never submits for you.
    6. Start the next contribution and click the bookmark again. It remembers which trip is next.
    7. Come back here and click Mark them all as done.
    If a field doesn't fill, most likely Vehicle, pick it yourself. The bookmark tells you which fields it couldn't do.

    How to use it

    A 2½-minute walkthrough, then the steps written out.

    The video isn't next to this file. Keep Mileage Tracker - How To.mp4 in the same folder as the tracker to watch it here.

    The Oracle screen in the video is an illustration. The real one looks slightly different, but the fields are the same.

    Set up once

    1. In Settings, type your car registration exactly as Oracle shows it.
    2. Add places you go often, such as Home and Office. They become quick buttons when you log a trip.
    3. Click Save settings.
    4. On the Oracle page, drag the red Fill Oracle Mileage button onto your bookmarks bar. Press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bar is hidden.

    Log a trip

    1. Open New trip. Today's date is filled in, so change it if the trip was earlier.
    2. Choose the journey type. The choices match Oracle.
    3. Enter the start and where you went. Tap a saved place or pick from the suggestions. Add more stops if you need them.
    4. Turn on Return journey if you drove back.
    5. Click Work out miles from route, or type the miles. Check in Google Maps opens the same route if you want to compare.
    6. Add the reason. Route details are written for you.
    7. Click Save trip. Doing the same journey again? Click repeat on it in the Log.

    Put trips into Oracle

    1. On the Oracle page, click Copy trips waiting to go into Oracle.
    2. In Oracle, open a new Mileage Personal Contribution.
    3. Click the Fill Oracle Mileage bookmark. Allow it to read what you copied, or paste with Ctrl+V if it asks.
    4. Check every field, then click Continue and Submit yourself.
    5. For the next trip, open a new contribution and click the bookmark again.
    6. When they're all in, click Mark them all as done in the tracker.

    Keep a backup

    Your trips live in this browser only. Once a month, go to Settings and click Download backup. Save the file somewhere safe, like OneDrive. Use Restore backup to bring trips back or move them to another computer.

    Something not working?

    The bookmark says to open a Personal Contribution first

    You're on a different Oracle screen. Open the form with Journey Date, Vehicle and Journey Type, then click the bookmark again.

    Vehicle didn't fill in

    Pick it from Oracle's list. If it isn't there, Oracle needs a Maintain Vehicle Details record with an up-to-date insurance expiry date, approved by your line manager. Also check the registration in Settings matches Oracle exactly.

    Nothing happens when I click the bookmark

    Your work computer may block bookmarks that run code. Ask IT, or copy the details across by hand from the Oracle page here.

    The wrong trip came up next

    Copy your trips again here, then click the bookmark in Oracle and choose Cancel to load the fresh list.

    The address I want isn't suggested

    Addresses come from Ordnance Survey, which has every UK address. Places in Lancashire and nearby come first. If the one you want isn't listed, type a bit more of it, add the town, or type the postcode, which lists every house in it.

    No address suggestions, or a route service error

    Check you're online, or type the miles yourself. If it keeps happening, the built-in route key may have reached its daily limit. Try again tomorrow.

    My trips have disappeared

    You're probably in a different browser or on a different computer, or browsing data was cleared. Go to Settings and use Restore backup with your latest backup file.

    The miles differ from Google Maps

    The tracker uses the quickest route and Google may pick another. Use whichever figure your manager checks against.

    Are the £ amounts what I'll be paid?

    They're estimates at HMRC rates: 45p a mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year, then 25p. Oracle works out the actual payment.
